Good to Know

September timing

Ideal shoulder season — crowds thin after Aug 31, weather warm (22–28°C in Italy, 25°C in Lisbon), and prices dip from summer peaks.

Hyatt Regency Lisbon location

The hotel is in Belém — beautiful riverside setting but ~20 min from the historic center. The E15 tram runs along the waterfront into the city. Uber is fast and cheap for evenings in Alfama or Chiado.

Amex FHR tip

Book Taormina via AmexTravel.com. Add your hotel loyalty number at booking — you still earn hotel points despite booking through a third party.

Day 10 train (Wed 23 Sep)

The Intercity from Salerno to Taormina-Giardini is direct — the carriages are loaded onto a ferry to cross the Messina Strait. You stay in your seat the whole way. Aim for a mid-morning departure from Salerno. Book on trenitalia.com up to 4 months ahead.

Amalfi Coast transport

From Positano, short ferries run to Amalfi town, Ravello, and Salerno. Much easier and more scenic than the winding cliff roads — use the boats whenever possible.

Trevi Fountain tip

Go at dusk or very early morning — dramatically fewer crowds. A €2 ticket gives access to the lower platform for an up-close view worth every cent.
